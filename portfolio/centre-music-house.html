The story

  • When I was in elementary school I took guitar and drum lessons at Centre Music House. When I noticed their lack of an online presence, I saw a full-circle moment waiting to happen.
  • I've worked with Centre Music House since March 2025.
  • I got in the door by offering a free one-month trial.

What I did

Four ways I helped

Marketing Strategy

Restarted their accounts with a mix of education, comedy, and event promo/recap posts. I balanced attracting new students with engaging our current community.

Social Media Management

Built an inbound funnel with educational content on Instagram and Facebook. I set up systems to post consistently, without causing a headache for the staff.

Content Creation

Planned, filmed, and edited content that stayed true to the brand. Worked closely with teachers, students, and the CMH owners to bring it to life.

Community Development

Featured CMH students to strengthen their connection to the school and kept the community in the loop on upcoming events. I stayed engaged through comments and DMs to build real relationships.

Lead magnet + landing page

A parent's guide that turns followers into leads

Here's the funnel. We offer parents a free guide they actually want — a simple roadmap for starting their kid in music. A dedicated landing page captures their interest and email. That turns CMH's everyday audience into real leads the store can follow up with, instead of likes that go nowhere.
